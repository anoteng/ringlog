How to install

  1. Download the APK file above to your Android device.
  2. Open your device Settings → Apps → Special app access → Install unknown apps and allow your browser or file manager to install APKs.
  3. Open the downloaded ringlog.apk file and tap Install.
  4. Log in with your existing RingLog account.

What's included

  • View all your flocks and birds
  • Daily egg log with light hours and bedding tracking
  • Hatch progress with timeline and progress bar
  • Bird detail with notes and photos
  • Access to shared flocks

Full management (creating flocks, editing settings, reports) is available at ringlog.no.
